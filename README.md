🧠 The Problem

Engineering Drawing is still taught using manual tools — large sheets, compasses, T-squares, and drafters. Students struggle with accuracy, portability, and revision because the process is slow, physical, and hard to redo.
While tools like AutoCAD or Fusion 360 exist, they’re too complex for early learners, focusing more on 3D modeling than 2D projection fundamentals.
There’s a gap between “manual drawing” and “CAD software” — and no digital bridge exists for students to learn foundational drawing skills in an interactive, intuitive way.

💡 The Solution — LineAccurate

LineAccurate brings the manual drawing board online.
It’s a web-based drafting environment where students can practice drawing lines, projections, and geometric constructions with precision, undo freedom, and visual clarity — just like on paper, but better.

Key features:

✏️ Digital Drawing Board: Practice projections, lines, and geometric shapes interactively.

📏 Smart Tools: Use ruler, compass, and protractor equivalents digitally.

🧭 Accuracy Feedback: Real-time feedback on alignment and proportions.

💻 Zero Setup: No downloads, runs on any browser.

🎓 Beginner Friendly: Perfect for students before they jump to complex CAD tools.

🎯 Why LineAccurate Matters

Helps students practice anywhere, without carrying tools.

Bridges the gap between manual drawing & AutoCAD-level tools.

Makes foundational design learning fun, fast, and interactive.

Perfect for first-year engineering and design students who struggle with manual precision but aren’t ready for CAD yet.
